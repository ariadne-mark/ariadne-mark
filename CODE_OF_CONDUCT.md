# Community code of conduct

We welcome contributors and users of all backgrounds. Be respectful, specific, and constructive, including when disagreeing about technical or regulatory questions.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Respect consent and use only appropriate, authorized media in examples and tests.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, and restrict participation when needed. Decisions should be proportionate and based on behavior, not disagreement or identity.

For private conduct concerns, use the maintainer contact listed in the organization profile. Do not post sensitive details in public issues. If no private contact is available, use GitHub's Report abuse mechanism on the relevant content or account.
