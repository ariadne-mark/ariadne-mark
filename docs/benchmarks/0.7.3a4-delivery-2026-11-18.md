# Current-release delivery transformations · 0.7.3a4

Measured **18 November 2026** on the unchanged qualified Linux CPU image.

Ordinary JPEG/H.264 recompression and the listed resizes retained the expected ID in all four scenes tested for each applicable profile. A three-second excerpt lost Economy5s in all four scenes; Economy2s recovered two, had one unconfirmed observation and one completed negative; Resilient recovered four. Cropping already-marked landscape video to 9:16 lost the ID in all twelve cases. These are observed counts, not estimated probabilities or a legal pass threshold.

## Protocol and scope

- Four retained stock scenes: face, crowd, mixkit-1164 and mixkit-5016. Four extracted 1024×576 PNG frames and four 1280×720 SDR H.264 clips at 24 fps, 8.96–10 seconds. Source and prepared-input hashes, stream metadata and preparation commands are retained.
- One new ID per scene/profile, no repeated trials: four image exports and twelve video exports, then 96 verification observations. Rows and profiles share source scenes and are dependent; do not pool them into a durability percentage.
- m6a.large, 2 vCPU / 8 GiB host; container limited to 2 CPU / 4 GiB, two inference threads. Runtime source `49578688710a8bae2373aaf9d47ff9521f566d4c`, immutable image `sha256:1a4dd358bcf698df654daaac631a26d3bc874104de5108bc94ce941c33a49980`.
- Encoder and verifier ran in separate sessions. Verification used a read-only recovery registry, `verification_scope="current"`, no signing key and no expected-ID/profile hint. The harness compared the report to the receipt only after verification.
- Ordinary transforms use Pillow or FFmpeg. Exact codec, color, scaling and seek commands are in the archive. This is not a test of a named social platform, HEVC/HDR delivery or an adversarial remover.
- Engineering fixtures, not actual AI-generated outputs. Fixture provenance claims identify a test system and must not be reused as production generation claims. The purpose is to observe mechanics of the current exporter/verifier.
- Container CPU counters include Python, Node and FFmpeg; per-observation wall time and memory are retained. The first operation includes startup and later checks reuse a worker. This campaign is not a replacement for the isolated cold/warm performance benchmark.

## Practical use

Keep Economy5s for workflows that can retain its marking windows; do not describe it as reliable for arbitrary short excerpts. Economy2s adds opportunities but did not recover every tested excerpt. Resilient improved excerpt survival in this small cohort and still failed the landscape-to-portrait crop. Prepare the final aspect ratio before marking, and test the application's actual export/CDN path. Existing modes and defaults are unchanged.

There were no wrong confirmed IDs and no incomplete searches in this cohort. That is not a population false-positive estimate: this campaign has no held-out negative corpus. Broader footage, durations, frame rates, crop locations and randomized excerpt offsets, current remover tests and independent production CA/TSA trust remain separate work. Historical cohorts remain labelled with their original versions.
