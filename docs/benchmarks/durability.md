# Durability testing and evidence

Use the [current protocols](protocols.md) and [versioned evidence index](evidence-index.md)
for methodology, metrics and actual results. Durability/error-rate qualification
remains partial; a fresh export gate is different from later transform survival.

The [original harness](../../benchmarks/durability/README.md) and
[0.2 results](../validation/0.2.0a0.md) remain historical smoke evidence. The
legacy VideoSeal benchmark is not an Economy benchmark. Current 0.7.2a1 kit
acceptance proves metadata-only PNG/MP4 recovery and distinct schedules, not
new recompression, crop, removal or population accuracy results.

Keep source counts, IDs, transform parameters, complete/incomplete outcomes,
wrong IDs, hardware, cold/warm policy and raw logs. No 90%/95% threshold is a
statutory pass mark. Measure product-specific ordinary transforms and plausible
threats; retain failures and operator decisions.
