# AI labeling for a small generative startup

If you generate images or video on your own backend, put labeling at the final
export boundary. Ariadne combines the watermark, signed provenance, verifier
and recovery registry so you can evaluate one connected implementation.

## Start with four decisions

| Decision | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Your role | Model/system provider, downstream integrator or deployer; a small team does not by itself decide legal applicability |
| Your output | Created or materially altered visual media; final dimensions/codec, original or synthetic audio, existing upstream credentials |
| Your markets and dates | EU and California coverage/exceptions/effective dates from the maintained jurisdiction guides, plus any other markets you actually serve |
| Your delivery | Every crop/resize/encode, where visible disclosure belongs, and how a recipient will reach the verifier |

## Go from evaluation to deployment

Run the [downloadable first-export walkthrough](../start-here.md). Pick a video
mode based on your content, especially short clips and excerpts. Deploy the
verifier/registry, supply your actual signing identity and configure trust/TSA.
Test the published bytes downloaded through your own app/CDN. Keep the receipt,
configuration, software versions and transformation results with your release.

The machine-readable mark and user-facing label solve different obligations.
One does not automatically replace the other. The SDK does not review your
terms, rights to source material, privacy, reporting channel or business role.
Use the [requirements matrix](../compliance/requirements-matrix.md) to distinguish
implemented measures, tested behavior and your remaining actions.
