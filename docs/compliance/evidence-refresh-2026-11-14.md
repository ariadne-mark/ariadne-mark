## Dates and source revisions

The official Commission reproduction of [Article 111(4)](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-111)
gives providers of systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 until
2 December 2026 for Article 50(2). It does not postpone a deployer's applicable
deepfake disclosures. The planner already distinguishes these roles and dates.

The final voluntary [Code](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/code-practice-ai-generated-content)
Measure 3.4(c) specifies 2 February 2027 for its detection-interoperability
milestone. It is a milestone on the chosen Code route, not statutory approval
of a particular SDK. An adequate route may be selected; implementing every
provider detector is not required by that measure.

The retrieved official guidelines PDF is
[C(2026)7702 final, 5 November 2026](https://ec.europa.eu/newsroom/dae/redirection/document/131215),
not just the original publication date of its landing page. Paragraphs 71–88
discuss marking, detection, feasibility and cost. No numerical frame cadence
or 90/95% statutory safe harbor was established in the reviewed texts.

California's chaptered SB1000, effective 30 September 2026, removes the
covered-provider monthly-user threshold and the former offer of a manifest
disclosure. Latent disclosure, no-cost verification, privacy, feedback and
relevant licensing processes remain. This amended provider scope is already
in the planner. Other actor duties have separate dates. EU cost language does
not override California's latent-disclosure durability wording.

R4 source CI and review qualify 0.7.3a1 at 52abf6e, not a later revision.
The completed adoption emulator pilot used 0.7.2a1: 21 video exports and
96 verification checks including 13 intact controls. Of 83 modified checks,
80 recovered the expected ID. Economy5s missed three tested excerpts/short
windows. This is a small scoped corpus, not a population error rate or proof
that earlier portrait-crop, letterbox or removal failures are fixed.

Economy5s default, Economy2s and Resilient remain available. Select using actual
delivery evidence and compute cost. The candidate's accompanying exact-version
qualification records state which checks were run; older speed figures are
not relabelled as new candidate measurements.

Supply truthful publisher/system/version/action/time fields; deploy the free
verifier/API and registry with an accessible product entry point; maintain
privacy, feedback and continuity. Review applicable deepfake UI disclosures
and synthetic audio separately. A successful own-registry lookup does not
prove full provider-agnostic interoperability. Retain an independent client
and external-validator check with their exact scope and versions.

The [authenticated TSA route](../operations/authenticated-timestamping.md)
enables a bearer credential on the pinned pair. Offline test PKI is separate
from enrollment, real timestamp-signer trust and live operator qualification.
