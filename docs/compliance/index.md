# AI labeling requirements and implementation evidence

Source review **2026-11-10** · SDK **0.7.2a1** visual-media scope.

Start with the role your product plays, its output types and where it is
available. Then connect each applicable marking/detection duty to a technical
measure, evidence and an accountable operator action.

- [EU Article 50](eu-ai-act.md): provider marking/detection, relevant deployer
  disclosure and the specific transition by market-placement date.
- [California](california.md): current covered-provider latent disclosure,
  public verification, privacy and feedback duties.
- [Requirement matrix](requirements-matrix.md): binding law, voluntary Code
  measures and engineering policy shown separately.
- [Official excerpts](source-excerpts.md): short original fragments, source
  authority and edition/review dates.
- [EU Code mapping](eu-code-map.md): available components, partial evidence
  and remaining route-specific actions.
- [Operator evidence template](operator-evidence-template.md): product facts,
  configuration, delivery tests and decisions to retain for your release.

Ariadne supplies watermarking, signed C2PA provenance, a verifier/registry and
signed reports. You configure truthful public facts, production trust and
operation, applicable disclosures and tests of your own delivery workflow.
Read the [integration guide](../implementation/integration.md) and
[production onboarding](../operations/production-onboarding.md).

Scope is JPEG/PNG and supported SDR MP4. Synthetic audio, independent text,
marking-only upstream relay and full provider-agnostic watermark discovery are
not complete in this visual kit. A passing engineering check is evidence of
that check, not an automatic product-wide legal determination.
