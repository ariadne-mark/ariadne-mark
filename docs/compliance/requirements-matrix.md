## Evidence and use

The [0.7.2a1 qualification](../validation/0.7.2a1-results.md) binds exact SDK
artifacts to 217 installed-media tests, the core matrix, clean installs and
kit integration. [Historical observations](../benchmarks/evidence-index.md)
retain their original versions/corpora. They do not prove population error rates,
statutory permanence or current production throughput.

Machine-readable copies: [CSV](requirements-matrix.csv) and [JSON](requirements-matrix.json).
The repository's 15 [implementation requirement records](../../compliance/requirements.json)
are a separate engineering tracker, not 15 statutory obligations. Open operator
or evidence work remains open even where the implementing component exists.
