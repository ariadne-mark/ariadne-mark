## Authority and version

The EU Act and California Code are binding law. The Commission Guidelines
explain implementation; they are not an amendment of Article 50. The voluntary
Code is a recognised practical framework, not identical to the statute or a
product certificate. C2PA is a technical standard; TrustMark and the three video
modes are implementation choices. A trusted TSA on every production export and
a +5-minute issuance allowance are SDK policies, not statutory frame/time thresholds.

For Guidelines, keep distinct the page's initial publication date (20 July 2026),
its update (5 November 2026), and the inspected PDF's edition (Brussels,
5 November 2026, C(2026) 7702 final). The current Code PDF was inspected by
measure number; no edition date is inferred from a filename.
